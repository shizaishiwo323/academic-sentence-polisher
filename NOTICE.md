# Notice

This repository contains an independently written, transformative scientific-writing workflow.

Its design was informed in part by the pedagogical framework in:

Hilary Glasman-Deal, *Science Research Writing for Non-Native Speakers of English*, Imperial College Press, 2010.

The original book, its PDF, chapter text, exercises, answer keys, and extended examples are not included. The materials in this repository paraphrase general writing principles, reorganize them into a Codex workflow, and add original routing, scientific-integrity checks, Chinese-to-English translation guidance, manuscript-level auditing, target-journal adaptation, output contracts, and evaluation cases.
